# Calculating with complex numbers

A complex number is often denoted $z$. In the complex number $z = a + bi$,
$a$ is called the **real part of $z$** and $b$ the **imaginary part of $z$**. The real part
of $z$ is written $\operatorname{Re} z$ and the imaginary part of $z$ is written
$\operatorname{Im} z$. So for the number $z = 3 - 2i$ we have
$\operatorname{Re} z = 3$ and $\operatorname{Im} z = -2$.

Addition, subtraction and multiplication of complex numbers are calculated with the
rules we already know: we treat $i$ like any other number and finally use
$i^2 = -1$.

For a complex number $z = a + bi$, the complex number $\bar{z} = a - bi$ is
its **conjugate**. So the conjugate of a complex number is obtained by changing
the sign of the imaginary part.

When dividing complex numbers we always multiply the numerator and denominator by the conjugate of the denominator.
The product $(a + bi)(a - bi) = a^2 + b^2$ is real, so we get a
division by a real number and can continue from there.

::: formel "Real part, imaginary part and conjugate"
For a complex number $z = a + bi$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Re} z &= a\\[2pt]
\operatorname{Im} z &= b\\[2pt]
\bar{z} &= a - bi
\end{aligned}
$$
:::

::: exempel "Example 1: Addition, subtraction and multiplication"
**Calculate<br>a)&nbsp;$(7 + 2i) + (5 - 6i)$&emsp;&emsp;b)&nbsp;$(7 - i) - (10 - 3i)$&emsp;&emsp;c)&nbsp;$(3i + 8)(2i - 5)$**

::: handskrift
typ: komplexarakna
:::

::: textlosning
**a)** We remove the brackets and keep all the signs inside the brackets.

$$
(7 + 2i) + (5 - 6i) = 7 + 2i + 5 - 6i = 12 - 4i
$$

**b)** We remove the brackets and change the signs inside the bracket that has
a minus sign in front.

$$
(7 - i) - (10 - 3i) = 7 - i - 10 + 3i = -3 + 2i
$$

**c)** We multiply out the brackets and use $i^2 = -1$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(3i + 8)(2i - 5) &= 3i\cdot 2i - 3i\cdot 5 + 8\cdot 2i - 8\cdot 5\\
&= 6i^2 - 15i + 16i - 40\\
&= -6 - 15i + 16i - 40 = -46 + i
\end{aligned}
$$

**Answer:** a) $12 - 4i$&emsp;&emsp;b) $-3 + 2i$&emsp;&emsp;c) $-46 + i$
:::
:::

::: exempel "Example 2: Division by multiplying by the conjugate"
**Calculate<br>a)&nbsp;$\dfrac{5 + 3i}{4 - 2i}$&emsp;&emsp;b)&nbsp;$\dfrac{9 - 4i}{i}$**

::: handskrift
typ: komplexdivision
:::

::: textlosning
**a)** We multiply the numerator and denominator by the conjugate of the denominator, $(4 + 2i)$. Then the denominator
becomes real.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{5 + 3i}{4 - 2i} &= \frac{(5 + 3i)(4 + 2i)}{(4 - 2i)(4 + 2i)}
= \frac{20 + 10i + 12i + 6i^2}{16 - 4i^2}\\[4pt]
&= \frac{20 + 22i - 6}{16 + 4} = \frac{14 + 22i}{20}
= \frac{14}{20} + \frac{22}{20}i = 0.7 + 1.1i
\end{aligned}
$$

**b)** We want to get rid of imaginary numbers in the denominator. Here it is enough to multiply
the numerator and denominator by $i$ (multiplying by $-i$ would also have worked well).

$$
\frac{9 - 4i}{i} = \frac{i(9 - 4i)}{i\cdot i} = \frac{9i - 4i^2}{i^2}
= \frac{9i + 4}{-1} = -9i - 4 = -4 - 9i
$$

**Answer:** a) $0.7 + 1.1i$&emsp;&emsp;b) $-4 - 9i$
:::
:::

::: exempel "Example 3: Find a complex number by comparing parts"
**Solve the equation $2\operatorname{Re} z - z = 5 - 3i$.**

::: handskrift
typ: identifikation
:::

::: textlosning
Since we do not know which complex number it is, we write it in general
form as $z = a + bi$. Then $\operatorname{Re} z = a$ and
$\operatorname{Im} z = b$. This gives

$$
2a - (a + bi) = 5 - 3i
$$

We simplify and then find $a$ and $b$ by comparing parts (comparing
the left-hand side and the right-hand side):

$$
2a - a - bi = 5 - 3i \quad\Longrightarrow\quad a - bi = 5 - 3i
$$

Comparing parts gives $a = 5$ and $b = 3$. Substituting into $z = a + bi$ gives
$z = 5 + 3i$.

**Answer:** $z = 5 + 3i$
:::
:::

::: sammanfattning "Summary"

::: sampunkt "Notation"
- A complex number is often denoted $z = a + bi$.
- $\operatorname{Re} z = a$ is the real part and $\operatorname{Im} z = b$ is the imaginary part.
- The imaginary part is the **number** $b$, not $bi$. For $z = 3 - 2i$, $\operatorname{Im} z = -2$.
:::

::: sampunkt "Conjugate"
- The conjugate of $z = a + bi$ is $\bar{z} = a - bi$.
- So you change the sign of the imaginary part.
- The product $(a + bi)(a - bi) = a^2 + b^2$ is **real**. That is the whole point.
:::

::: sampunkt "The four operations"
- **Addition and subtraction:** the real parts separately and the imaginary parts separately.
- **Multiplication:** multiply out the brackets and use $i^2 = -1$.
- **Division:** multiply the numerator and denominator by **the conjugate of the denominator**, so that the denominator becomes real.
- If the denominator is just $i$, it is enough to multiply by $i$.
:::

::: sampunkt "Comparing parts"
- If the number is unknown: write it in general form $z = a + bi$.
- Simplify the equation and compare the left-hand side with the right-hand side.
- The real parts must be equal, and so must the imaginary parts. That gives $a$ and $b$.
:::

:::
