# Writing and interpreting expressions

Renting a studio costs a fixed fee of 800 kr and then 150 kr per
hour. The total cost of renting the studio for a certain number of hours can
then be put together in a table:

| Number of hours | Total cost (kr) |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | $800 + 150 \cdot 1$ |
| 2 | $800 + 150 \cdot 2$ |
| 3 | $800 + 150 \cdot 3$ |
| 5 | $800 + 150 \cdot 5$ |
| $x$ | $800 + 150 \cdot x$ |

We can write an expression for the cost of renting the studio for *x* hours as
$800 + 150 \cdot x$ or $800 + 150x$.

We will now look at the expression $800 + 150x$ and go through what the different
parts are called. Learn the terms in bold in the right-hand column!

::: formel "The parts of an expression"
| Content | Explanation | Term |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $x$ | the letter | **variable** |
| 150 | "the number in front of $x$", the number that multiplies the variable | **coefficient** |
| $150x$ | the whole term that contains the variable | **variable term** |
| 800 | the term that consists only of a number | **constant term** |
:::

We calculate the cost of renting the studio for, say, 12 hours by
setting $x = 12$ and substituting into the formula $800 + 150x$:

$$
800 + 150 \cdot 12 = 800 + 1\,800 = 2\,600
$$

So the cost is 2 600 kr, where 2 600 is called the **value** of the expression.

::: exempel "Example 1: Writing expressions for age"
**Linn is $x$ years old. Albin is 3 times as old as Linn was 4 years ago.
Write an expression for<br>a) Linn's age 12 years ago&emsp;&emsp;b) Albin's age.**

::: handskrift
typ: tecknaalder
:::

::: textlosning
**a)** If Linn is $x$ years old now, we must subtract 12 to get her age
12 years ago.

**Answer:** $x - 12$

**b)** Linn is $x$ years old now. 4 years ago she was $(x - 4)$ years old. Albin is
therefore 3 times that, that is, Albin is $3(x - 4)$ years old.

We multiply a factor in front of brackets by each term inside:

$$
3(x - 4) = 3x - 12
$$

**Answer:** $3(x - 4)$ or $3x - 12$
:::
:::

::: exempel "Example 2: Interpreting expressions"
**Apples cost $a$ kr each and pears $b$ kr each. Interpret<br>a)&nbsp;$3a + 4b$&emsp;&emsp;b)&nbsp;$10a - 7b$**

::: handskrift
typ: tolkauttryck
:::

::: textlosning
**a)** Three apples cost $3a$ kr and four pears cost $4b$ kr. The sum is
the total cost.

**Answer:** The cost of 3 apples and 4 pears.

**b)** The difference compares the price of 10 apples with the price of 7 pears.

**Answer:** The price difference between 10 apples and 7 pears.
:::
:::

::: exempel "Example 3: Calculating the value of an expression"
**Calculate the value of $20 - 3x$ if<br>a)&nbsp;$x = 4$&emsp;&emsp;b)&nbsp;$x = -5$**

::: handskrift
typ: uttryckvarde
:::

::: textlosning
**a)** Replace $x$ with 4 in the expression and calculate:

$$
20 - 3 \cdot 4 = 20 - 12 = 8
$$

**Answer:** 8

**b)** Replace $x$ with −5. Remember the sign rules (minus times minus gives
plus):

$$
20 - 3 \cdot (-5) = 20 + 15 = 35
$$

**Answer:** 35
:::
:::

::: sammanfattning "Summary"

::: sampunkt "The parts of an expression"
- **Variable**: the letter, for example $x$.
- **Coefficient**: the number in front of the variable, the one that multiplies it.
- **Variable term**: the whole term that contains the variable, such as $150x$.
- **Constant term**: the term that is just a number, such as 800.
:::

::: sampunkt "Writing an expression"
- Look for what is **fixed** (becomes the constant term) and what depends on the number
  (becomes the variable term).
- Studio rent with 800 kr fixed and 150 kr per hour becomes
  $800 + 150x$.
:::

::: sampunkt "The value of an expression"
- Substitute a number for the variable and calculate.
- $x = 12$ in $800 + 150x$ gives 2 600. That result is called the
  **value** of the expression.
- Take care with **signs**: $20 - 3x$ with $x = -5$ becomes
  $20 + 15 = 35$.
:::

::: sampunkt "Words that become operations"
- "times as old" means multiplication.
- "4 years ago" means subtraction, so $x - 4$.
- If it concerns a whole changed quantity: use **brackets**, such as
  $3(x - 4)$.
:::
:::
