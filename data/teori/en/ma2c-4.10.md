# Congruence

When similar figures are the same size, there is **congruence** and we say
that the figures are congruent. Congruence is shown with the sign ≅ and
is read "is congruent to".

"The triangle $ABC$ is congruent to the triangle $DEF$" can therefore be written

$$
\triangle ABC \cong \triangle DEF
$$

To decide whether two triangles are congruent, we do not need to investigate
all the angles and sides; it is enough that one of the three
congruence conditions below is satisfied.

::: tips "Congruent triangles"
Two triangles are congruent if they match in

- two sides and the included angle (side-angle-side or **SAS**)
- all three sides (side-side-side or **SSS**)
- two angles and the included side (angle-side-angle or
  **ASA**)
:::

In figures, sides of equal length are marked with the same number of tick marks, and
equal angles with angle arcs.

::: textlosning
The triangles are congruent by SSS.

Then

$$
\angle D = \angle A \quad \text{(the angle opposite the line segment marked with two tick marks)}
$$

$$
\angle E = \angle B = 48° \quad \text{(the angle opposite the line segment marked with three tick marks)}
$$

$$
\angle F = \angle C = 102° \quad \text{(the angle opposite the line segment marked with one tick mark)}
$$

and the last angle is obtained from the angle sum:

$$
\angle D = 180° - 48° - 102° = 30° \quad \text{(angle sum of a triangle)}
$$

**Answer:** $\angle D = 30°$, $\angle E = 48°$ and $\angle F = 102°$

::: sammanfattning "Summary"

::: sampunkt "Congruence"
- The figures are **similar and the same size**.
- Written with the sign $\cong$:
  $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle DEF$.
- The scale factor is thus 1.
:::

::: sampunkt "The three congruence conditions"
- **SAS**: two sides and the **included** angle.
- **SSS**: all three sides.
- **ASA**: two angles and the **included** side.
- It is enough that **one** of them is satisfied.
:::

::: sampunkt "The word included is crucial"
- The angle must lie **between** the two sides, and the side **between** the
  two angles.
- Otherwise the condition does not apply.
:::

::: sampunkt "Markings in the figure"
- Sides of equal length: **the same number of tick marks**.
- Equal angles: **the same number of angle arcs**.
- Pair up sides and angles according to the markings, not according to how the figure
  happens to be rotated.
:::

::: sampunkt "What you may use"
- In congruent triangles **all** corresponding sides and angles are equal.
- An angle lies **opposite** its corresponding side. That connection
  is used to find the right angle.
:::
:::
