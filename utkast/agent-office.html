      How the agent office works

      Fysiklabbet is built by a teacher and a team of AI agents. Each figure in the office is an agent with its own instruction file under .claude/agents/: its own role, its own rules and its own tools. No agent may publish anything on its own without passing review.

      These events are not made up

      Everything that happens in the office is replayed from real logs from the chosen day: Claude Code's session transcripts (every delegation to an agent, every verifier run, commit and push), the git history and the logs from the scheduled jobs. The time on the clock is the real time when it happened. The clock runs slowly when something is happening and fast-forwards when the office is quiet.

      The site is Swedish, so commit messages and task titles are shown in Swedish, as they were written.

      The workflow

      • The teacher gives a task. Claude (the main agent) takes it and delegates parts to the specialists: the educator reviews the physics, the proofreader the language, the illustrator the figures.
      • Everything passes through the verification gate, some twenty automatic checks of typography, figures, language and page structure, before it is saved and pushed to main, which is the published site.
      • Every night at 03:15 the scheduler wakes the news editor, who writes today's physics news. The publisher checks it against the original study and rules APPROVED, FIX FIRST or STOPPED.
      • At lunchtime and in the evening, posts are published on Facebook and Instagram. The social media watch checks that they went out, reruns the jobs and raises an alarm otherwise.
      • The reply agent and the newsletter editor only write drafts. It is always the teacher who sends.
