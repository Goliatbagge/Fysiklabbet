# Simplifying expressions

When we write an expression in a simpler way, it is called **simplifying
expressions**.

::: formel "Simplifying expressions"
We can only add **like terms** together, that is, $x$-terms
on their own, $y$-terms on their own, $x^2$-terms on their own, constant terms on their own,
and so on. Pay close attention to the sign in front of each term!
:::

::: exempel "Example 1: Collecting like terms"
**Simplify<br>a)&nbsp;$8x + 3y - 2x + 9y$&emsp;&emsp;b)&nbsp;$5x^2 + 3x - x^2$**

::: handskrift
typ: likatermer
:::

::: textlosning
**a)** We collect the $x$-terms on their own and the $y$-terms on their own. We see that
the $x$-terms give $8x - 2x = 6x$ and the $y$-terms give $3y + 9y = 12y$:

$$
8x + 3y - 2x + 9y = 6x + 12y
$$

**Answer:** $6x + 12y$

**b)** We can only add $x^2$-terms on their own and $x$-terms on their own:

$$
5x^2 + 3x - x^2 = 4x^2 + 3x
$$

**Answer:** $4x^2 + 3x$
:::
:::

::: formel "Addition and subtraction with expressions in brackets"
When a

- **plus sign** stands in front of brackets, the brackets can be removed *without
  changing anything inside the brackets*:<br>
  $a + (b + c) = a + b + c$&emsp;&emsp;$a + (b - c) = a + b - c$
- **minus sign** stands in front of brackets, the brackets can be removed if you
  at the same time change the signs of the terms inside the brackets (plus signs become
  minus signs and minus signs become plus signs):<br>
  $a - (b + c) = a - b - c$&emsp;&emsp;$a - (b - c) = a - b + c$
:::

::: exempel "Example 2: Minus sign in front of brackets"
**Simplify $20x - (5 + 3x - 7y)$.**

::: handskrift
typ: minusparentes
:::

::: textlosning
The minus sign in front of the brackets means that all the signs inside the brackets change
when the brackets are removed:

$$
20x - (5 + 3x - 7y) = 20x - 5 - 3x + 7y = 17x - 5 + 7y
$$

**Answer:** $17x - 5 + 7y$
:::
:::

::: exempel "Example 3: The perimeter of the rectangle"
**Write a simplified expression for the perimeter of the rectangle below.**

::: figur
<svg viewBox="16 4 206 118" width="261" height="149" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" font-family="Poppins, system-ui, sans-serif" role="img" aria-label="A rectangle with height x and width 2x plus 3."><rect x="20" y="8" width="180" height="84" fill="#9ccfae" stroke="#4f7d47" stroke-width="1.4"/><text x="208" y="55" font-size="15" fill="#1f2530"><tspan font-style="italic">x</tspan></text><text x="110" y="114" font-size="15" text-anchor="middle" fill="#1f2530">2<tspan font-style="italic">x</tspan> + 3</text></svg>
:::

::: handskrift
typ: omkrets
:::

::: textlosning
Since opposite sides of a rectangle are equal in length, the left side must
be $x$ and the top side must be $2x + 3$. The perimeter is found by adding
all the sides, that is, as

$$
(2x + 3) + (2x + 3) + x + x = 2x + 3 + 2x + 3 + x + x = 6x + 6
$$

**Answer:** $6x + 6$
:::
:::

::: sammanfattning "Summary"

::: sampunkt "Simplifying expressions"
- Writing the same expression in a simpler way.
- Only **like terms** may be added together: $x$-terms on their own,
  $y$-terms on their own, $x^2$-terms on their own and constant terms on their own.
- $x$ and $x^2$ are **not** like terms.
:::

::: sampunkt "Look at the sign"
- The sign in front of a term belongs to that term.
- $8x + 3y - 2x + 9y = 6x + 12y$.
:::

::: sampunkt "Plus sign in front of brackets"
- The brackets can be removed **without changing anything**.
- $a + (b - c) = a + b - c$
:::

::: sampunkt "Minus sign in front of brackets"
- The brackets can be removed if **all the signs inside are changed**.
- $a - (b + c) = a - b - c$
- $a - (b - c) = a - b + c$
- This is the most common source of errors in the whole section.
:::
:::
