# Loans and interest calculations

If we do not have money we can take out a **loan**. A loan can be given by a bank,
a lending company or a friend. Of course we have to pay the loan back.
Paying back a loan is called **amortising** (repaying). What we have to pay extra
can be said to be the price of the loan and is called **interest**. Interest is often given
as a certain percentage of the debt and is then called the **interest rate**.

Remember: If a task just says "interest", it is understood that
they mean the annual interest. Sometimes quarterly interest appears, where a quarter is a
"quarter of a year", that is 3 months.

::: formel "Paying off a loan"
When we pay off a loan we pay **amortisation** (repayment) *and*
**interest** (the cost of the loan).
:::

::: exempel "Example 1: The car loan"
**Ellen takes out a loan of 150 000 kr to buy a car. The repayment period
is 5 years and she repays equal amounts every month ("straight-line
amortisation"). The annual interest rate on the loan is 4.8 %. How<br>
a) large is the amortisation each month?<br>
b) much must she pay in total at the first payment?<br>
c) much will she pay in total for the loan?**

::: handskrift
typ: billan
:::

::: textlosning
**a)** If she is to repay every month for 5 years, she will make
$5 \cdot 12 = 60$ payments. If she is to repay the same amount
at each payment, the amortisation each month is

$$
\frac{150\,000\ \mathrm{kr}}{60} = 2\,500\ \mathrm{kr}
$$

**Answer:** 2 500 kr

**b)** At the first payment her debt is 150 000 kr.
The annual interest is 4.8 % of it, so

$$
\text{annual interest} = 0.048 \cdot 150\,000\ \mathrm{kr} = 7\,200\ \mathrm{kr}
$$

Since she pays monthly, she has to pay $\dfrac{1}{12}$ of this
amount in interest each month (there are 12 months in a year), so

$$
\text{interest} = \frac{7\,200\ \mathrm{kr}}{12} = 600\ \mathrm{kr}
$$

We found the amortisation each month to be 2 500 kr in part a). At the
first payment she must therefore pay

$$
\text{interest} + \text{amortisation} = 600\ \mathrm{kr} + 2\,500\ \mathrm{kr} = 3\,100\ \mathrm{kr}
$$

**Answer:** 3 100 kr

through the steps one at a time in the spreadsheet below. Below the sheet it is explained what
happens in each step.

**Answer:** 168 300 kr

::: sammanfattning "Summary"

::: sampunkt "The concepts"
- **Loan**: money you are allowed to borrow and must pay back.
- **Amortise**: to pay back **the loan itself**.
- **Interest**: the price of borrowing, that is the cost on top.
- **Interest rate**: the interest expressed as a percentage of the debt.
:::

::: sampunkt "What you pay each time"
- Each payment consists of **amortisation plus interest**.
- The interest is always calculated on the **remaining debt**.
:::

::: sampunkt "Straight-line amortisation"
- The same **amortisation amount** each time:
  $\dfrac{\text{the loan}}{\text{number of payments}}$.
- The debt decreases, so **the interest part becomes smaller each time**.
- That is why the **first** payment is the largest and the last the smallest.
:::

::: sampunkt "Calculating with monthly payments"
- The annual interest rate is divided by 12 to get the monthly interest rate.
- A quarter is three months, that is a quarter of a year.
:::

::: sampunkt "Total cost"
- The sum of all the payments, that is the loan plus all the interest.
- A loan of $150\,000$ kr at 4.8 % interest over 5 years costs in total
  about $168\,300$ kr.
:::
:::
