# How to use your calculator

Check that you can do the most common calculations on your calculator.
Try keying in the calculations below and check that you get the correct answer on
your calculator.

::: formel "Test your calculator"
$$
\sqrt{7} \approx 2.65
\qquad
\sqrt[3]{10} \approx 2.15
\qquad
\sqrt[8]{543} \approx 2.20
$$

$$
1.5^9 \approx 38.44
\qquad
45^{2/3} \approx 12.65
\qquad
35^{-2} \approx 0.00082
$$

$$
(-3.7)^2 = 13.69
$$
:::

## Brackets in the numerator and denominator

When the whole denominator (or numerator) consists of several numbers, it must be written
in brackets on the calculator, otherwise the calculator gets it wrong:

$$
\frac{360}{2 \cdot 3} = 60
$$

On the calculator: `360/(2·3)`. Without the bracket it is interpreted as
$\dfrac{360}{2} \cdot 3 = 540$.

## Answering as a fraction

Many calculators can answer in exact fraction form instead of decimal form. Check
that your calculator can simplify

$$
\frac{17}{51} = \frac{1}{3}
$$

## Interpreting the calculator's powers of ten

When numbers become very large or very small, the calculator shows them with
E notation, where E stands for "times 10 to the power of":

$$
3.7\mathrm{E}12 = 3.7 \cdot 10^{12} = 3\,700\,000\,000\,000
$$

$$
2.5\mathrm{E}{-4} = 2.5 \cdot 10^{-4} = 0.000\,25
$$

::: sammanfattning "Summary"

::: sampunkt "Roots and powers on the calculator"
- You should be able to find the square root, cube root and $n$th root:
  $\sqrt{7} \approx 2.65$ and
  $\sqrt[3]{10} \approx 2.15$.
- Powers, even with fractional and negative exponents:
  $45^{2/3} \approx 12.65$ and
  $35^{-2} \approx 0.00082$.
:::

::: sampunkt "Brackets in the numerator and denominator"
- If the denominator consists of several numbers, it **must** be written in brackets.
- $\dfrac{360}{2 \cdot 3} = 60$ is keyed in as `360/(2·3)`.
- Without the bracket the calculator works out
  $\dfrac{360}{2} \cdot 3 = 540$.
:::

::: sampunkt "Sign and bracket"
- $(-3.7)^{2} = 13.69$. Type the bracket, otherwise only the
  number is raised and the minus sign ends up outside.
:::

::: sampunkt "Answers in fraction form"
- Most calculators can give exact answers as fractions instead of decimals:
  $\dfrac{17}{51} = \dfrac{1}{3}$.
:::

::: sampunkt "E notation"
- **E means "times ten to the power of"**.
- $3.7\mathrm{E}12 = 3.7 \cdot 10^{12}$.
- $2.5\mathrm{E}{-4} = 2.5 \cdot 10^{-4}$.
- **Never** write E in an answer. Rewrite in standard form.
:::
:::
