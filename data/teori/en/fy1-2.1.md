# Average speed

The mean [[speed|The magnitude of velocity, without direction (a scalar). Measured in m/s or km/h.]] over a time interval is called the **[[average speed|Total distance travelled divided by total time. Has no direction (a scalar).]]**,
written *v*ₘ, and its [[SI unit|The International System of Units, with seven base units (m, kg, s, A, K, mol, cd).]] is m/s (metres per second). Average speed and
[[average velocity|The mean velocity over a time interval: the displacement Δ*s* divided by the time Δ*t*.]] are not the same thing, which we look at more closely later in this chapter.

::: formel "Average speed"
$$
v_\mathrm{m} = \frac{s}{t}
$$

where

- *v*ₘ = average speed (m/s)
- *s* = [[distance|The total length of path an object has moved. A scalar quantity, measured in metres.]] (m)
- *t* = time (s)
:::

## Converting units: m/s and km/h

::: formel "Converting units: m/s and km/h"
The conversion factor between m/s and km/h is 3.6.

$$
\mathrm{m/s} \xrightarrow{\;\times 3.6\;} \mathrm{km/h}
\qquad
\mathrm{km/h} \xrightarrow{\;\div 3.6\;} \mathrm{m/s}
$$

::: härledning "Derivation"
Another common unit for [[velocity|Change of position per unit time, with direction (a vector). SI unit m/s.]] is km/h, so it is useful to be able
to switch quickly between the two. We do a unit analysis.

$$
1\ \mathrm{km/h} \;=\; \frac{1\ \mathrm{km}}{1\ \mathrm{h}}
\;=\; \frac{1\,000\ \mathrm{m}}{3\,600\ \mathrm{s}}
\;=\; \frac{1}{3.6}\ \mathrm{m/s}
$$

This means that to convert from km/h to m/s we **divide** by
3.6. Conversely, to convert from m/s to km/h we
**multiply** by 3.6. We say that the conversion factor is 3.6.
:::
:::

::: exempel "Example 1: Converting between km/h and m/s"
**Convert<br>a) 90 km/h to m/s&emsp;&emsp;b) 20 m/s to km/h.**

::: handskrift
typ: kmhms
:::

::: textlosning
**a)** $\displaystyle 90\ \mathrm{km/h} = \frac{90}{3.6}\ \mathrm{m/s} = 25\ \mathrm{m/s}$

**Answer:** 25 m/s

**b)** $20\ \mathrm{m/s} = 20 \cdot 3.6\ \mathrm{km/h} = 72\ \mathrm{km/h}$

**Answer:** 72 km/h
:::
:::

::: exempel "Example 2: Falköping to Skövde"
**The speed limit on the road is 80 km/h. How much time do you save
by driving at an average speed of 90 km/h instead of 80 km/h on the
30 km stretch between Falköping and Skövde?**

::: handskrift
typ: falkoping
:::

::: textlosning
We write down the formula for average speed and solve for the time.

$$
v_\mathrm{m} = \frac{s}{t} \quad\Leftrightarrow\quad t = \frac{s}{v_\mathrm{m}}
$$

$$
\left[ \begin{array}{l}
s = 30\ \mathrm{km} = 30\,000\ \mathrm{m} \\
v_{\mathrm{m}1} = 90\ \mathrm{km/h} = \dfrac{90}{3.6}\ \mathrm{m/s} = 25\ \mathrm{m/s} \\
v_{\mathrm{m}2} = 80\ \mathrm{km/h} = \dfrac{80}{3.6}\ \mathrm{m/s} = 22.222\ldots\ \mathrm{m/s}
\end{array} \right]
$$

So the time it takes to drive the stretch at 90 km/h is

$$
t_1 = \frac{30\,000}{25} = 1\,200\ \mathrm{s}
$$

The time it takes to drive the stretch at 80 km/h is

$$
t_2 = \frac{30\,000}{22.222\ldots} = 1\,350\ \mathrm{s}
$$

So the time saved is

$$
\Delta t = t_2 - t_1 = 1\,350 - 1\,200 = 150\ \mathrm{s} = 2.5\ \text{minutes}
$$

**Answer:** 2.5 minutes (so the time saved is very small: keep to the speed limit!)
:::
:::

::: exempel "Example 3: The banana slug"
**The banana slug is said to be the slowest animal in the world. Its top speed
is 0.0023 cm/s. How far can it travel at most in one hour?**

::: handskrift
typ: banansnigel
:::

::: textlosning
We write down the formula for average speed and solve for the distance.

$$
v_\mathrm{m} = \frac{s}{t} \quad\Leftrightarrow\quad s = v_\mathrm{m} \cdot t
$$

$$
\left[ \begin{array}{l}
v_\mathrm{m} = 0.0023\ \mathrm{cm/s} = \dfrac{0.0023}{100}\ \mathrm{m/s} = 0.000\,023\ \mathrm{m/s} \\
t = 1.0\ \mathrm{h} = 60\ \mathrm{min} = 60 \cdot 60\ \mathrm{s} = 3\,600\ \mathrm{s}
\end{array} \right]
$$

$$
s = 0.000\,023 \cdot 3\,600 = 0.0828\ \mathrm{m} \approx 0.083\ \mathrm{m} = 8.3\ \mathrm{cm}
$$

**Answer:** 8.3 cm
:::
:::

::: sammanfattning "Summary"

::: sampunkt "Average speed"
- The mean speed over a time interval.
- $v_\mathrm{m} = \dfrac{s}{t}$, where $s$ is the distance (m) and $t$ the time (s).
- The SI unit is m/s. Average speed is a **scalar**: it has no direction.
:::

::: sampunkt "Converting m/s and km/h"
- The conversion factor is **3.6**.
- From km/h to m/s: **divide** by 3.6.
- From m/s to km/h: **multiply** by 3.6.
- $90\ \mathrm{km/h}$ = $25\ \mathrm{m/s}$ and $20\ \mathrm{m/s}$ = $72\ \mathrm{km/h}$.
:::

::: sampunkt "Where the 3.6 comes from"
- $1\ \mathrm{km/h} = \dfrac{1\,000\ \mathrm{m}}{3\,600\ \mathrm{s}}
  = \dfrac{1}{3.6}\ \mathrm{m/s}$.
:::

::: sampunkt "Solve for what you are looking for"
- Distance: $s = v_\mathrm{m} \cdot t$.
- Time: $t = \dfrac{s}{v_\mathrm{m}}$.
- Convert all values to SI units **before** substituting, that is metres
  and seconds.
:::
:::
